👤 Subscriber Guide

Learn how to subscribe to premium channels and groups

🔍 Finding Channels

Get an Invite Link

Receive an invite link from a channel owner or friend. The link looks like: https://t.me/YourBot?start=channel-id

Click the Link

Tap the invite link to open the MySubHub webapp. You'll see the channel details including price and duration.

Review Channel Info

Check the channel name, rating, reviews, subscription price, and duration before subscribing.

💳 Subscribing to a Channel

Connect Your Wallet

Click "Pay with TON" and connect your TON wallet (Tonkeeper, Tonhub, or other TON Connect compatible wallets).

Confirm Payment

Review the payment details including the subscription price, platform fee (1%), and network fee. Confirm the transaction in your wallet.

Wait for Confirmation

After payment, wait for the transaction to be confirmed on the blockchain (usually 10-30 seconds).

Access the Channel

Once confirmed, you'll be automatically added to the channel/group. You can now access all premium content!

📋 Managing Your Subscriptions

View Active Subscriptions

Open the dashboard and go to "My Subscriptions" tab to see all your active subscriptions.

Check Expiration Dates

Each subscription shows the expiration date. You'll receive reminders before expiration.

Renew Subscriptions

Click "Renew" on expired subscriptions to extend your access. The payment process is the same as initial subscription.

⭐ Rating and Reporting

Rate Channels

After subscribing, you can rate the channel from 1 to 5 stars. Your rating helps other users make informed decisions.

Report Issues

If you encounter scams, inappropriate content, or other issues, use the "Report" button to notify the platform admins.

💡 Pro Tip: Always check the channel rating and reviews before subscribing. Channels with higher ratings are more reliable.
⚠️ Important: Subscriptions are non-refundable. Make sure you understand the channel content and price before subscribing.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get access?

After payment confirmation, you'll be added to the channel within 10-30 seconds. If it takes longer, check your subscription status in the dashboard.

What happens when my subscription expires?

You'll be automatically removed from the channel. You can renew anytime by clicking the "Renew" button in your subscriptions list.

Can I get a refund?

Subscriptions are non-refundable. Please review the channel details carefully before subscribing.

What if I don't get access after payment?

Wait up to 1 minute for the transaction to confirm. If you still don't have access, contact support through the bot with your transaction hash.

Can I subscribe to multiple channels?

Yes! You can subscribe to as many channels as you want. Each subscription is managed independently.

What wallets are supported?

Any TON Connect compatible wallet including Tonkeeper, Tonhub, OpenMask, and others.

✅ Need Help? If you have any questions or issues, use the /help command in the bot or contact support.
