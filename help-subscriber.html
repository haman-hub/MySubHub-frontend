Subscriber Guide

Learn how to use MySubHub as a subscriber

1️⃣

Browse Channels

Discover premium Telegram channels and groups that offer exclusive content.

  • Channels are listed in the main dashboard
  • Each channel shows price, duration, and ratings
  • Click on a channel to see more details
2️⃣

Subscribe with TON

Pay securely using your TON wallet to access premium content.

  • Connect your TON wallet (Tonkeeper, Tonhub, etc.)
  • Review the subscription price and duration
  • Confirm the payment in your wallet
  • You'll be automatically added to the channel
3️⃣

Access Premium Content

Once subscribed, you'll have full access to the channel's content.

  • Click "Open" button on your subscription card
  • You'll be redirected to the Telegram channel
  • Enjoy exclusive content during your subscription period
4️⃣

Rate & Review

Help other users by rating channels you've subscribed to.

  • Click the "⭐ Rate" button on your subscription
  • Give a rating from 1 to 5 stars
  • Leave a comment about your experience
  • Your review helps others make informed decisions
5️⃣

Earn Referral Credits

Invite friends and earn 10% credits on their subscriptions!

  • Go to the "Referrals" tab
  • Copy your unique referral link
  • Share it with friends
  • Earn 10% credits when they subscribe
  • Use credits for your own subscriptions

💡 Note: Credits can only be used for subscriptions and cannot be withdrawn as TON.

6️⃣

Manage Subscriptions

Keep track of your active subscriptions and renewals.

  • View all subscriptions in the "My Subscriptions" tab
  • See expiration dates and renewal options
  • Click "🔄 Renew" to extend your subscription
  • Expired subscriptions show in red with renewal option

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when my subscription expires?

You'll be removed from the channel automatically. You can renew anytime by clicking the "Renew" button.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by individual channel owners. Contact the channel owner directly for refund requests.

How do referral credits work?

When someone subscribes using your referral link, you earn 10% of the subscription price as credits. These credits can be used for your own subscriptions.

Is my payment secure?

Yes! All payments are made directly through TON blockchain. We never hold your funds. The channel owner receives 99% and the platform takes 1% fee.
