Channel Owner Guide

Learn how to monetize your Telegram channel

1️⃣

Add Bot as Administrator

First, add @MySubsHub_bot to your channel as an administrator.

  • Open your Telegram channel
  • Go to Channel Settings → Administrators
  • Click "Add Administrator"
  • Search for @MySubsHub_bot
  • Grant ALL permissions (important!)
  • Click "Save" or "Done"

⚠️ Important: The bot needs full admin permissions to manage subscribers and verify payments.

2️⃣

Register Your Channel

Add your channel to MySubHub platform.

  • Go to "My Channels" tab
  • Click "➕ Add Channel Now"
  • Fill in channel details:
    • • Channel name
    • • Invite link
    • • Subscription price (in TON)
    • • Duration (1 week to unlimited)
  • Click "Add Channel"
3️⃣

Connect Your Wallet

Connect your TON wallet to receive payments.

  • Click on wallet icon in the top right
  • Connect your TON wallet (Tonkeeper, Tonhub, etc.)
  • Your wallet address will be saved automatically
  • All payments will go directly to your wallet

💰 Revenue Split: You receive 99% of subscription fees. Platform takes 1% fee.

4️⃣

Share Your Channel

Promote your channel and attract subscribers.

  • Go to "My Channels" tab
  • Click "📋 Copy" to copy subscription link
  • Or click "📤 Forward" to share with template message
  • Share in social media, other channels, etc.
  • Users click the link to subscribe

Example subscription link:

https://t.me/MySubsHub_bot?start=your-channel-id
5️⃣

Manage Subscribers

The bot automatically manages your subscribers.

  • When user pays → Bot adds them to channel
  • When subscription expires → Bot removes them
  • Users can renew with one click
  • Track active subscribers in dashboard

🤖 Automatic: Everything is automated! You don't need to manually add/remove users.

6️⃣

Track Earnings

Monitor your revenue and subscriber stats.

  • View total subscribers count
  • See active vs expired subscriptions
  • Track total earnings
  • Monitor channel ratings and reviews
7️⃣

Withdraw Earnings

Request withdrawal of your accumulated earnings.

  • Go to "Withdrawals" section
  • Enter withdrawal amount (minimum 1 TON)
  • Submit withdrawal request
  • Admin processes your request
  • Funds sent to your connected wallet

⏱️ Processing Time: Withdrawals are processed manually by admin. Please allow 24-48 hours.

8️⃣

Edit Channel Settings

Update your channel's price and duration anytime.

  • Go to "My Channels" tab
  • Click "⚙️ Edit" on your channel
  • Update subscription price
  • Change duration if needed
  • Click "Save Changes"

💡 Note: Changes apply to new subscriptions only. Existing subscriptions keep their original terms.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much do I earn?

You receive 99% of subscription fees. The platform takes a 1% fee to cover operational costs.

When do I receive payments?

Payments go directly to your connected TON wallet instantly when users subscribe.

Can I have multiple channels?

Yes! You can register up to 5 channels per account.

What happens if a subscriber requests a refund?

Refunds are handled directly between you and the subscriber. The platform doesn't process refunds automatically.

Can I change the subscription price?

Yes! You can update your price anytime. Changes apply to new subscriptions only.
