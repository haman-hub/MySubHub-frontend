📺 Channel Owner Guide

Learn how to monetize your Telegram channel or group

🚀 Getting Started

Add the Bot as Admin

Go to your channel/group → Click on channel name → Administrators → Add Administrator → Search for @MySubsHub_bot → Add as admin with "Invite Users" permission.

Bot Detects Your Channel

Once added, the bot will automatically detect your channel and send you a confirmation message with a link to configure your channel.

Open Configuration Dashboard

Click the link in the bot message or use the /mychannels command to open your channel management dashboard.

⚙️ Configuring Your Channel

Set Subscription Price

Click "Edit" on your channel and set the subscription price in TON. Choose a price that matches your content value.

Set Subscription Duration

Choose the subscription duration (e.g., 30 days, 90 days, 365 days). This determines how long subscribers have access.

Activate Your Channel

Toggle the "Active" switch to enable your channel. Once active, users can subscribe and you'll start receiving payments.

Connect Your Wallet

Connect your TON wallet to receive payments. Click "Connect Wallet" and select your wallet (Tonkeeper, Tonhub, etc.).

💰 Understanding Revenue

Revenue Distribution

When a user subscribes, the payment is split as follows:

Example: 1 TON subscription

Total paid by user: 1.05 TON
├─ Your earnings (99%): 0.99 TON
├─ Platform fee (1%): 0.01 TON
└─ Network fee: 0.05 TON

You receive: 0.99 TON

Track Earnings

View your pending earnings in the dashboard. Earnings show the total amount available for withdrawal.

Request Withdrawal

Click "Request Withdrawal" to transfer your earnings to your wallet. Withdrawals are processed manually by the admin team.

📢 Promoting Your Channel

Copy Invite Link

Click "Copy Link" to get your unique invite link. Share this link to attract subscribers.

Forward to Contacts

Click "Forward" to share your channel with contacts. The message includes channel details, rating, and a direct subscribe link.

Auto-Post Welcome Messages

Enable auto-post to send personalized welcome messages when users subscribe. Edit the template to include your custom message.

🏆 Getting Verified

Request Verification

Click "Verify" on your channel card. Provide evidence such as social media links, website, or channel statistics.

Admin Review

The admin team will review your submission. Verification usually takes 24-48 hours.

Get Verified Badge

Once verified, your channel will show a blue checkmark badge, increasing credibility and attracting more subscribers.

💡 Pro Tip: Channels with verification badges and high ratings get 40% more subscribers. Focus on quality content and encourage satisfied subscribers to leave reviews.
⚠️ Important: Keep your channel content appropriate and follow Telegram's terms of service. Channels that receive multiple reports may be suspended.
✅ Auto-Post Template Variables: Use {user} for subscriber name, {channel} for channel name, and {date} for subscription date.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive payments?

Connect your TON wallet in the dashboard. When users subscribe, 99% of the payment goes to your wallet. Request withdrawals anytime.

How long do withdrawals take?

Withdrawals are processed manually by the admin team. Processing time is typically 24-48 hours.

Can I change the price later?

Yes! You can update your subscription price anytime. Price changes only apply to new subscribers.

What if a subscriber requests a refund?

Subscriptions are non-refundable. Make sure your channel description clearly explains the content and value.

Can I have multiple channels?

Yes! You can add and manage multiple channels. Each channel has its own settings and earnings.

What if my channel is reported?

The admin team will review the report. If the report is valid, your channel may be suspended. Maintain quality content to avoid reports.

How do I get more reviews?

Encourage subscribers to leave reviews by providing excellent content. You can mention this in your channel posts.

What happens when a subscription expires?

The subscriber is automatically removed from your channel. They can renew anytime to regain access.
