Connect your bot to MySubHub backend in one click

📋 How to use this tool:

  1. Copy your Bot Token from @BotFather
  2. Verify the Backend URL is correct
  3. Enter your Webhook Secret (from backend env vars)
  4. Click "Set Webhook"
  5. Open your bot in Telegram and send /start

⚠️ Important: Bot Privacy

  1. Open @BotFather on Telegram
  2. Send /setprivacy
  3. Select your bot
  4. Choose DISABLED

⚡ This is REQUIRED for the bot to receive channel events!
